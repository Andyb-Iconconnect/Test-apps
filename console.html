Identity

          

            MMSI is the only field live tracking actually needs — AIS broadcasts on
            MMSI and never on IMO, and there is no free way to look one up from the
            other. It comes off the ship's radio licence or her AIS unit. The IMO
            number is checked against its own check digit, so a mistyped one is
            caught here rather than three weeks later.
